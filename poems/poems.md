# Poems

## Morning Window

Light gathers slowly on the floor,  
and the room remembers how to wake.  
A sparrow stitches song to air,  
while yesterday loosens its weight.

## Small Compass

When roads divide beneath the rain,  
keep one kind thought close at hand.  
It will not name the distant shore,  
but it can help you understand.

## Night Garden

The moon leaves silver in the leaves,  
the flowers keep their quiet flame.  
In darkness, even hidden roots  
continue reaching just the same.
